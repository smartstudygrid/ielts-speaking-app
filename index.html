IELTS Band Descriptors At A Glance

Band 6.0: Willing to speak at length; may lose coherence at times; basic vocabulary suffices; mixed simple & complex sentences.
Band 7.0: Speaks effortlessly without noticeable effort; uses flexible vocabulary & idioms with some slips; frequent error-free complex sentences.
Band 8.0: Fluent with only rare hesitation; wide lexical resource with subtle nuances; wide range of complex structures consistently accurate.
